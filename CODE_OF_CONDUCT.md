# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Be considerate, constructive and welcoming across backgrounds, identities and experience levels. Discuss technical decisions with evidence, and respect the people making them.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in issues, pull requests or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions or restrict participation when needed. To raise a conduct concern, contact the maintainer through an available private GitHub channel; do not publish another person's private information. Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and in good faith.
